How to use

  1. Enter the amount you want to convert in the amount field at the top of the tool.
  2. Select your source currency (for example USD) and your target currency (for example EUR) from the dropdown menus.
  3. The converted result appears instantly. Use the swap button to reverse the currencies with one click.
  4. Remember these are indicative rates for quick estimates — banks and transfer services add their own fees and margins on real transactions.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — every tool on this site is 100% free with no limits, no signup and no watermarks.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

Are these live exchange rates?

No. The rates built into this tool are indicative rates meant for quick estimates and travel planning. Live mid-market rates move every second; for an actual money transfer, always check your bank's or transfer provider's quoted rate including fees.

Which currencies can I convert between?

The tool supports 12 major world currencies: US Dollar (USD), Euro (EUR), British Pound (GBP), Pakistani Rupee (PKR), Indian Rupee (INR), UAE Dirham (AED), Saudi Riyal (SAR), Chinese Yuan (CNY), Japanese Yen (JPY), Australian Dollar (AUD), Canadian Dollar (CAD) and Turkish Lira (TRY).

Why does my bank show a different rate?

Banks and money-transfer services add a spread (margin) on top of the mid-market rate, plus flat fees. The difference between the indicative rate here and your bank's rate is how they make money on the exchange — always compare the final amount you receive, not just the rate.

Does the converter work offline?

Yes. All conversion math runs locally in your browser, so the tool works without an internet connection. Note the rates are bundled reference values, so they won't reflect live market movements while you're offline.

Currency Converter — Complete Guide

Whether you're planning a trip abroad, shopping from an international store, or sending money to family, a quick currency conversion helps you understand what things really cost in your own money. This free currency converter turns any amount between 12 major world currencies instantly — right in your browser, with no signup and no data leaving your device.

How currency conversion works

Every conversion multiplies your amount by an exchange rate: the price of one currency expressed in another. If 1 USD = 278 PKR, then 50 USD converts to 13,900 PKR. Rates are quoted as pairs (USD/PKR, EUR/USD) and move constantly with global markets, interest-rate decisions, and economic news.

Indicative rates vs. real transaction rates

The rates in this tool are indicative — good enough for estimates, shopping comparisons, and travel budgeting. They are not the rate you'll get at a bank counter. Real transactions use either the mid-market rate (the midpoint between buy and sell prices, which you almost never get as a consumer) or a customer rate that includes the provider's margin. Banks typically add 1–4% over mid-market; airport kiosks can add far more. Money-transfer apps tend to be cheaper than banks but still take a cut.

Practical use cases

Travel budgeting: Convert hotel prices, meal costs, and tour fees into your home currency before you fly, so there are no surprises. Online shopping: That gadget priced in dollars or dirhams — what does it really cost you after conversion? Freelancing: If clients pay in USD, EUR, or AED, convert invoices to your local currency to plan your month. Education: Students comparing tuition fees across countries can normalize everything into one currency.

Tips for getting the best rate

Compare at least two providers before any large transfer — the cheapest headline rate isn't always cheapest after fees. Avoid exchanging cash at airports unless you must. For recurring transfers (salary, family support), look at specialist transfer services rather than your bank. And never make a big financial decision on an indicative rate alone: confirm the final credited amount in writing first.

Privacy note

Unlike bank apps and rate websites that log every conversion you run, this tool processes everything locally. Your amounts and currency pairs never touch a server — handy when you're converting salary figures or business amounts you'd rather keep private.
