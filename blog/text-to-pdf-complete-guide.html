Text to PDF: The Complete Guide (2026)

PDF is the world's most portable document format — it looks identical on every device, prints perfectly, and is accepted by virtually every institution, employer and government office. But creating PDFs usually means installing software or uploading sensitive documents to a random website. This guide shows you how to convert text to PDF (and images to PDF) completely free, right in your browser, with your files never leaving your device.

Why convert text to PDF?

  • Universal compatibility: A PDF looks the same on Windows, Mac, Android, iPhone and Linux. A Word file doesn't.
  • Professional appearance: Resumes, invoices, contracts and reports are expected as PDFs.
  • Print fidelity: What you see is what prints — no reflowed layouts or missing fonts.
  • Easy sharing: One file, no dependencies, opens everywhere.
  • Archiving: PDF/A is an ISO standard specifically designed for long-term document preservation.

Method 2: Browser print-to-PDF (for any text)

For text content — an article, a web page, a document you wrote — every modern browser has a built-in "Print to PDF" or "Save as PDF" option:

  • Chrome/Edge: Press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on Mac), set destination to "Save as PDF".
  • Firefox: Ctrl+P → "Print to PDF" or "Microsoft Print to PDF".
  • Safari: File → Export as PDF.
  • Android/iPhone: Share → Print → pinch out on the preview → Save as PDF.

Tip: enable "Background graphics" in the print dialog if your document has colors or images you want to keep.

PDF tips most people don't know

  • Compress before emailing: Most email providers cap attachments at 20–25 MB. Compress first, send once.
  • Order matters when merging: Arrange source files in final reading order before merging — reordering after is extra work.
  • Images → PDF quality: Higher-resolution source photos produce sharper PDFs. Photograph documents in good light, straight-on.
  • File naming: Use descriptive names (Invoice-2026-10.pdf, not scan001.pdf) — your future self will thank you.
  • Check before sending: Open the final PDF and skim every page. Merged documents sometimes have blank or duplicated pages.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a file size limit?

Because processing happens locally in your browser, limits depend on your device's memory rather than an arbitrary server cap. Most everyday documents convert in seconds.

Will my PDF work on all devices?

Yes — PDFs generated this way use standard formatting readable by every PDF viewer on every platform.

Can I convert a Word document to PDF?

Open the Word document and use your browser or office app's Print → Save as PDF function, or copy the text into a document and print to PDF. For scanned/image-based Word exports, use Images to PDF.
