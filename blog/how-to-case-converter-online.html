Title case vs. sentence case: which should you use?

These two get confused constantly. Title Case capitalizes (almost) every word and is the convention for headlines, book titles, slide headings and YouTube video titles — anywhere you want maximum prominence. Sentence case capitalizes only the first word and proper nouns, matching normal prose; it's increasingly preferred for UI buttons, email subjects and modern minimalist headings. Neither is "more correct" — pick the one your style guide or platform expects, and be consistent. When in doubt for a headline, Title Case is the safer, more traditional choice.
