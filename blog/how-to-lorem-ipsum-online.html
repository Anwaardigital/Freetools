How to Generate Lorem Ipsum Text Online for Free (2026 Guide)

Every designer has faced the same problem: the layout is ready, but the real content hasn't arrived yet. That is exactly what lorem ipsum is for — placeholder text that lets you judge a design's look and feel without distracting anyone with readable words. In this guide you'll learn how to generate lorem ipsum text online for free, step by step, plus the practical ways designers actually use it in real projects.

What is lorem ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is scrambled pseudo-Latin text used as a stand-in for real content. It traces back to a work by the Roman writer Cicero from 45 BC, De finibus bonorum et malorum ("On the Ends of Good and Evil"). The famous opening words "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet..." are a garbled version of a passage from that text. Printers and typesetters have used it since the 1960s, and it became the standard placeholder of the digital age.

The point of the scrambled Latin is psychological: because readers can't focus on the meaning of the words, they evaluate the visual design instead — the typeface, spacing, line length and hierarchy. If you used real English copy in a mockup, clients would read the text instead of judging the design.

When to use placeholder text (and when not to)

Real use cases where lorem ipsum shines:

  • Web design mockups: Fill hero sections, cards and article layouts before the copywriter delivers.
  • Typography testing: Compare fonts and sizes across a full page of realistic-looking text.
  • Wireframes and prototypes: Show clients how text blocks will behave without writing content yourself.
  • Print layouts: Judge column widths and page breaks in brochures and magazines.
  • CMS and template development: Seed test pages so empty databases don't mislead you.

When not to use it: in user testing. Participants who see lorem ipsum can't tell you whether the content answers their questions, so usability tests need real (or at least realistic) copy.

Tips for professional results

  • Match the expected content length. If the real article will be ~800 words, don't test with 3 paragraphs. Generate roughly the right volume so the layout survives when real copy lands.
  • Regenerate, don't reuse. If every mockup on your site shows the same block, a client may think content is duplicated. One click gives you a fresh block.
  • Pair with our text tools. Use the Word Counter to check the length of generated text, or the Character Counter when a design has strict character limits.
  • Replace before launch. This sounds obvious, but "lorem ipsum" has appeared on live websites more than once. Make removing it part of your pre-launch checklist.
  • For client presentations, mention it's temporary. A one-line note ("copy to come") prevents anyone mistaking placeholder text for a design decision.

How much text do I need?

A rough guide:

  • Hero subtitle or tagline: 5–12 words
  • Feature card description: 1–2 sentences
  • Blog excerpt: 2–3 sentences
  • About section: 2–4 paragraphs
  • Full article layout test: 8–12 paragraphs

Is placeholder text SEO-safe?

Yes — as long as it's temporary. Search engines only care about what's on the page when they crawl it, so replace all placeholder text with real, relevant content before publishing. A page full of lorem ipsum has no meaningful content to rank.
