Subscriber & Like Counter

Track your channel growth: how many days to your next subscriber goal, your like percentage, and your next milestone.

How to use

  1. Enter your current subscribers and how many new subscribers you gain per day.
  2. Enter your target subscriber count and click “Calculate Goal”.
  3. Enter likes and dislikes to see your like percentage and ratio.
  4. Check your next milestone to see how many more subscribers you need.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — every tool on this site is 100% free with no limits, no signup and no watermarks.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

Where does the subscriber data come from?

From the numbers you type in. This tool does not connect to YouTube, TikTok or any other service and cannot fetch a channel’s live count — it calculates from your own numbers.

How is the next milestone picked?

It is the next round number above your current count — 1,000, 5,000, 10,000, 50,000, 100,000, 500,000, 1 million and so on.

Complete Guide to Subscriber Goals and Like Ratios

Two numbers tell you how a channel is doing: how fast subscribers arrive, and how many viewers bother to like. Divide the gap to your goal by your daily growth and you get the days left — that is the whole goal calculation, and it works for any channel size. The like ratio is even simpler: likes divided by likes plus dislikes. Above 90% is healthy; below 80% usually means the video reached the wrong audience. Milestones work the same way: the next round number is the goal, and the gap is what you still need. None of this needs a live connection — type your own numbers and the answers are honest, because they come from your channel, not from a guess.
