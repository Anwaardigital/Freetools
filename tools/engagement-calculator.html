What is a good engagement rate?

On Instagram, 1–3% is normal and above 3% is strong. On TikTok, 5% or more is common. Small accounts usually score higher than big ones.

Engagement by followers or by views?

Both are used. By followers shows how loyal your audience is; by views shows how well a single post performed. This calculator shows both.

Complete Guide to Engagement Rate

Engagement rate is the share of people who did something — liked, commented, shared or saved — instead of just scrolling past. It is the number brands look at first, because it shows real attention, not just reach. A post with 1,000 views and 100 interactions has a 10% rate; a post with 100,000 views and 1,000 interactions has only 1%. Small accounts often win here: a loyal audience of a thousand beats a bored audience of a hundred thousand. Track your rate per post, not per month, and you will see exactly what your audience loves.
