Meme Maker

Make memes in seconds: upload an image, add top and bottom text, download the PNG.

How to use

  1. Upload an image from your device.
  2. Type your top text and bottom text.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — every tool on this site is 100% free with no limits, no signup and no watermarks.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

Can I use any image?

Use your own photos or images you have the right to use. Do not use someone else’s photo or artwork without permission.

Why is my text hard to read?

White text with a dark outline reads best on most photos. If your image is very bright, pick a darker photo or shorten the text.

Complete Guide to Making Memes

A good meme is one idea, in as few words as possible. The classic shape is simple: a picture everyone recognises, top text that sets up the situation, bottom text that delivers the twist. Short beats clever — if the joke needs explaining, cut words until it does not. This maker keeps it simple: upload any image, add top and bottom text, download the PNG. Make it about something your audience already feels, and the shares take care of themselves.
