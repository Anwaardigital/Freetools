Complete Guide to Fake Followers

Fake followers are easy to buy and hard to spot at a glance — but they leave signs. A profile with 50,000 followers and 3 likes per post is not popular, it is padded. Comments like “nice pic” on every post, empty bios, and usernames that are random letters and numbers are all warnings. For creators, fake followers hurt twice: brands check engagement before paying, and the platform shows your posts to fewer real people when most followers never interact. Use this checker before you collaborate with anyone or pay for promotion.
