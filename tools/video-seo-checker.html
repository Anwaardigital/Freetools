Does passing every check guarantee views?

No. These checks cover the basics — a clear title, a keyword in the first lines, enough tags. Good content and a good thumbnail still decide whether people click and stay.

What is the most important check?

Put your main keyword early — in the first half of the title and in the first two lines of the description. That is how search finds your video.

Complete Guide to Video SEO

Video SEO is simple at its core: help the platform understand your video, and help viewers decide to click. The title should contain the words people search for, the first two lines of the description should repeat the main idea (that is what shows in search), and tags should match the topic. Length matters too — very short descriptions give search nothing to work with, while a few honest sentences do the job. This checker runs your title, description and tags against those basics and tells you exactly what to fix before you publish.
