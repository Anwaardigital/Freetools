Estimate your monthly AdSense or YouTube earnings from views and RPM. See what your traffic is worth.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — every tool on this site is 100% free with no limits, no signup and no watermarks.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

What is RPM?

RPM is revenue per 1,000 views — what you actually earn for every thousand views or page views. It changes with your audience’s country, your topic and the season.

Are these numbers guaranteed?

No — this is an estimate. Real earnings depend on your RPM, which moves up and down. Use the result to plan, not as a promise.

Complete Guide to AdSense Earnings

AdSense and YouTube earnings follow one simple formula: views times RPM, divided by a thousand. The hard part is RPM — it is high for finance and technology audiences in rich countries, and low for entertainment audiences elsewhere. A channel with 100,000 monthly views at a $2 RPM earns about $200; at a $10 RPM the same views earn $1,000. That is why two channels with the same views can earn very different amounts. Use this estimator to see what your traffic is worth, then focus on growing the audience that advertisers pay most to reach.
