Text to Speech (Urdu)

Hear any text read aloud in Urdu or English. Adjust speed and pick a voice.

How to use

  1. Paste or type your text into the box.
  2. Pick a language and a voice.
  3. Set the speaking speed.
  4. Click “Speak” to hear it read aloud.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — every tool on this site is 100% free with no limits, no signup and no watermarks.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

Why does the Urdu voice sound robotic?

The voice comes from your device, not from this site. Quality depends on the voices installed on your phone or computer — newer devices usually sound better.

Can I download the audio?

This tool reads text aloud; it does not save an audio file. To record it, use your device’s screen recorder while it speaks.

Complete Guide to Text to Speech

Text to speech turns reading into listening — useful for proofreading your own writing, for long articles when your eyes are tired, and for anyone who prefers to hear words. Hearing your own draft read aloud is also the fastest way to catch mistakes: awkward sentences and repeated words jump out when spoken. Paste the text, pick a voice and a comfortable speed, and listen. Slow it down for study, speed it up for a quick review. The voices come from your device, so the same text can sound different on a phone and on a computer.
