Hashtag Generator

Generate relevant hashtags for Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and Facebook from any topic. Copy them in one click.

How to use

  1. Type your topic — for example “homemade biryani” or “travel vlog”.
  2. Pick the platform and how many hashtags you want.
  3. Click “Generate Hashtags”.
  4. Click “Copy All” and paste them into your post.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — every tool on this site is 100% free with no limits, no signup and no watermarks.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

How many hashtags should I use?

Instagram allows up to 30, but 10–15 focused tags usually work best. YouTube and TikTok do well with fewer — 5–10 relevant tags beat 30 random ones.

Will these hashtags make my post go viral?

No tool can promise that. Hashtags help the right people find your post — good content and consistency do the rest. Mix broad tags with specific ones for your niche.

Complete Guide to Hashtags

Hashtags are how platforms sort content, so the right tags put your post in front of people who already care about the topic. Broad tags like #travel reach millions but drown fast; specific tags like #karachifood reach fewer people but the right ones. This generator mixes tags from your exact words, your niche and your platform, then removes duplicates. Use it as a starting point — replace any tag that does not fit your post, and keep a small set of tags you reuse for your niche.
