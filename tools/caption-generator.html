Caption Generator

Write scroll-stopping captions for Reels, Shorts and posts. Pick a tone, enter a topic, get captions instantly.

How to use

  1. Type your topic — for example “morning chai” or “first day at gym”.
  2. Choose a tone: funny, motivational, professional or casual.
  3. Pick the platform you are posting on.
  4. Click a caption to copy it, then paste it under your post.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — every tool on this site is 100% free with no limits, no signup and no watermarks.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

Can I edit the captions?

Yes — treat them as a first draft. Change a word or two so the caption sounds like you; your own voice always performs better than a copied line.

Which tone works best?

It depends on your audience. Funny captions get shares, motivational ones get saves, professional ones build trust. Try one tone for a week and compare the response.

Complete Guide to Captions

A good caption does two jobs: it stops the scroll and it starts a conversation. The first line matters most — on Instagram only the first line shows before “more”, so put the hook there. A question at the end invites comments, and a clear call to action (“save this for later”) earns saves. This generator gives you five captions in your chosen tone for any topic. Use them as starting points, add one detail that only you could write, and keep your captions short enough to read in a few seconds.
