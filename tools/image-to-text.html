Image to Text (OCR)

Extract text from any image or photo with OCR. Upload an image and copy the text.

How to use

  1. Select an image with text — a photo, screenshot or scan.
  2. Pick the text language: English, Urdu or both.
  3. Click “Extract Text” and wait for the engine to load.
  4. Copy the extracted text from the box.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — every tool on this site is 100% free with no limits, no signup and no watermarks.

Is my data private?

Your image never leaves your device. The OCR engine is downloaded once from a CDN the first time you use the tool; the reading itself happens locally in your browser.

Why is some text wrong?

OCR reads pixels, not meaning. Blurry, tilted or handwritten text is misread most. A clear, well-lit, straight photo gives the best result — always check before copying.

Does it work on handwriting?

Poorly. Printed text works well; neat handwriting works sometimes; messy handwriting rarely works. For handwriting, a clear photo and short lines help.

Complete Guide to Image OCR

OCR — optical character recognition — turns a picture of text into text you can copy, search and edit. It is the fastest way to reuse words from a photo, a screenshot, a scanned page or a whiteboard. The engine first downloads to your browser (about 10 MB, once), then reads the image locally. Clear, straight, well-lit text works best; shadows, tilt and fancy fonts cause mistakes. Use it for notes, receipts, book pages and signs — and always read the result once before you paste it anywhere important.
