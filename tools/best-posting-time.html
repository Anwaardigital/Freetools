FAQ

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

Are these times the same for everyone?

No — they are general patterns based on when most people are online. Your own audience may be most active at different times; check your own insights after a few weeks.

Does posting time really matter?

It helps, but it is not the biggest factor. A good post at an average time beats an average post at the perfect time. Use the windows as a starting point.

Complete Guide to Posting Times

Posting when your audience is awake and scrolling gives a new post its best first hour — and the first hour decides how far a post travels. Most people check their phones in the morning, at lunch and in the evening, so those windows work for most niches. But a channel for night-shift workers or students will peak at different hours. Use the windows on this page as a starting point, post at the same times for a few weeks, then look at your own numbers and adjust. Consistency matters more than any single perfect minute.
