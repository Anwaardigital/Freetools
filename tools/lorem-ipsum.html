📜 Lorem Ipsum Generator

Generate placeholder paragraphs, sentences or words for designs and mockups. Useful for designers filling in draft pages. Free, unlimited, private — works entirely in your browser.

How to use

  1. Pick a type from the dropdown — Paragraphs, Sentences or Words.
  2. Set the Amount (1–50) to control how much placeholder text you need.
  3. Click Generate to create fresh text (a sample is generated automatically when the page loads).
  4. Click Copy to copy the result to your clipboard, then paste it into your mockup, wireframe or design tool.

Can I generate more than 50 at a time?

The amount goes up to 50 per click, and each paragraph contains 3–6 sentences, so 50 paragraphs is already a large document. If you need more, click Generate again and paste the batches together.

Lorem Ipsum Generator Guide

Lorem ipsum is the design industry's standard placeholder text — pseudo-Latin that looks like real copy but carries no meaning. It comes from Cicero's "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (45 BC), scrambled into nonsense in the 1500s by an unknown typesetter. Designers have used it ever since because it lets clients and teams judge a layout — spacing, typography, hierarchy — without getting distracted by readable content.

How this generator works

Choose Paragraphs, Sentences or Words and set the amount (1–50). Words are drawn at random from a bank of genuine Latin words, then assembled into sentences of 6–14 words each; paragraphs group 3–6 sentences together. Because the selection is random, every click produces a different result, so you will never get the same block twice — handy when you need several distinct dummy articles for one project.

Practical uses

Drop generated paragraphs into website mockups and landing-page wireframes to test how real content volume will feel. Use a few sentences to check heading-plus-body combinations, or a string of words to fill short labels and card titles. Developers use it to seed CMS templates and test databases, and print designers use it to preview brochures and PDFs before final copy is approved. Pair it with the Word Counter and Character Counter tools to verify your layouts hold the right amount of text.

Tips and common mistakes

Match the amount to reality: generating 20 paragraphs for a hero banner creates a misleading mockup. Start with the approximate real length — three paragraphs for a blog card, a single sentence for a tooltip. Never ship lorem ipsum to production: it looks professional in a mockup but embarrassing on a live page, so search your project for "lorem" before every launch. If you need the famous classic opening line, paste it at the top of your generated text manually. And when a client asks "what does it say?" — the answer is nothing, and that is exactly the point.
