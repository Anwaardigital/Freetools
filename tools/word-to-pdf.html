Word to PDF

Turn a Word (.docx) document into a clean PDF you can share anywhere.

How to use

  1. Select a Word (.docx) file from your device.
  2. Wait for the document to appear in the preview.
  3. Check that the pages look right.
  4. Click “Download PDF” or print to PDF.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — every tool on this site is 100% free with no limits, no signup and no watermarks.

Is my data private?

Your file never leaves your device. The document reader is downloaded once from a CDN the first time you use the tool; the conversion itself happens locally in your browser.

Will my formatting survive?

Mostly. Headings, bold text, lists and tables usually look right. Very complex layouts, unusual fonts and tracked changes may look different in the PDF.

Can I convert .doc (old Word) files?

This tool reads modern .docx files. For old .doc files, open them once in Word or Google Docs and save as .docx first.

Complete Guide to Word to PDF

PDF is the format for sharing: it looks the same on every device, it cannot be edited by accident, and anyone can open it. That is why job applications, forms and final reports are sent as PDF, not Word. This tool reads your .docx in the browser, shows you a preview of the pages, and saves a clean PDF you can send anywhere. Check the preview before downloading — if a table or heading looks wrong there, it will look wrong in the PDF too. Your file never leaves your device.
