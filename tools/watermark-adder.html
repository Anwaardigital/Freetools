FAQ

Is it really free?

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

What should my watermark say?

Your name, brand or website. Keep it short — one or two words read best on a photo.

Will the watermark stop theft?

It discourages casual copying, but a determined person can crop or cover it. Put it somewhere visible but not over the main subject, and keep your original files safe.

Complete Guide to Watermarks

A watermark is a quiet signature on your photo: it says who made it, and it makes casual copying pointless. The best watermarks are readable but not loud — your name or brand in white with a dark outline, placed in a corner or across the middle at low opacity. Too faint and it is useless; too strong and it ruins the photo. This tool adds a text watermark in your browser: pick the words, the position, the size and the opacity, then download the PNG. Your original photo is never changed or uploaded.
