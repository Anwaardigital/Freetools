🔗 Link Shortener

Turn long, ugly URLs into short, shareable links in one click. Free, unlimited, no signup.

How to use

  1. Paste your long URL in the box below.
  2. Click "Shorten Link" — your short link appears instantly.
  3. Copy it with one click and share anywhere.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — every tool on this site is 100% free with no limits and no signup.

How does it work?

Your URL is sent to a free shortening service (da.gd / is.gd) over a secure connection, which returns a compact link. Nothing is stored on this site.
