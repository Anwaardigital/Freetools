Tag Extractor

Pull the tags out of any YouTube video or block of text. Paste a link or text and copy the tag list.

How to use

  1. Paste a YouTube video URL, or paste a block of text.
  2. Click “Extract Tags”.
  3. Review the tag list — duplicates are removed automatically.
  4. Click “Copy Tags” and use them in your own video.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — every tool on this site is 100% free with no limits, no signup and no watermarks.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

Should I copy a big channel’s tags?

Use them to learn what a successful video targets — then write your own tags for your own video. Copying tags word for word does not copy the channel’s audience.

Do tags still matter on YouTube?

Less than they used to — the title, description and thumbnail matter more. But good tags still help the platform understand what your video is about.

Complete Guide to Video Tags

Tags are small labels that tell YouTube what your video is about. The best tags are the exact words people search for: your main topic, close variations of it, and the name of your niche. A video about biryani might use “biryani recipe”, “chicken biryani” and “easy biryani”. This tool pulls the tags from any video so you can see what others target. Use the list as research, then choose tags that honestly describe your own video — misleading tags hurt more than they help.
