Link Safety Checker

Check a link before you click it. Flags shorteners, lookalike domains and suspicious patterns.

How to use

  1. Paste the link you received.
  2. Click “Check Link”.
  3. Read the warnings: shortener, lookalike domain, strange characters.
  4. If anything looks wrong, do not click — type the real site address yourself.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — every tool on this site is 100% free with no limits, no signup and no watermarks.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

Can this tool guarantee a link is safe?

No. It checks the address for known warning signs — it does not open the link or scan the site behind it. A clean result means “no warning signs found”, not “definitely safe”.

What is a lookalike domain?

A fake address that looks like a real one — for example “faceb00k.com” with zeros, or “arnazon.com” with a swapped letter. Scammers use them to steal passwords.

Complete Guide to Checking Links

Most scams start with one click. A message promises a prize, a job or a parcel, and the link goes to a copy of a real site that steals your password. Before you click any link you did not expect, check it: hover to see the real address, look for shortened links that hide the destination, and watch for lookalike spellings. This tool flags the common warning signs in seconds. The safest habit is simple — for banks, parcels and accounts, never use the link in a message; type the address yourself or open the official app.
