FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — every tool on this site is 100% free with no limits, no signup and no watermarks.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

How long should a bio be?

Short. Instagram shows about 150 characters, and most people read only the first line. One line about what you do and one line about why to follow you is enough.

Can I use emojis in my bio?

You can, but use one or two at most. Too many emojis make a bio hard to read, and screen readers read every emoji name aloud.

Complete Guide to Bios

A bio is read in two seconds, so every word must earn its place. Say what you do, who it is for, and why someone should follow you — in that order. “I teach home cooking to busy beginners” tells a visitor more than “Food lover | Dreamer | Chai addict”. Add one link or call to action, and update your bio whenever your focus changes. This generator turns a few words about you into bio drafts; pick the closest one, then edit it until it sounds like you.
