Privacy Policy

Your files never leave your device. Every tool on Free Privacy Tools runs 100% in your browser using JavaScript. We do not upload, store, or see your files.

No accounts. There is no signup or login. We use anonymous Google Analytics to understand site usage — it collects no names, emails, or file contents.

Analytics & consent. We use Google Analytics (gtag.js, ID G-M0NYM1FT9H) to understand anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (such as pages visited and device type). It loads only after you press "Accept" on our cookie banner — if you decline, no analytics runs. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. No personal data is collected, stored, or shared.

Advertising. We may display ads via Google AdSense to keep the site free. Ad providers may use cookies per their own policies.

Third-party services. Most tools run fully offline in your browser, but a few need the internet and query third-party APIs over HTTPS: ipapi.co (IP Lookup — geolocation of the address you enter), api.hackertarget.com (Security Headers Checker — fetches response headers of the site you enter), and URL-shortening services (da.gd, is.gd, v.gd, tinyurl.com — the URL you ask to shorten is sent to the service), with api.allorigins.win used only as a fallback connection helper for the shortener. When you use these tools, the data you type (an IP address, a domain, a URL) is sent to that third party so it can answer your request. We do not control these providers’ privacy practices — check their policies if you are unsure.

Contact. For questions, reach us via the site footer links.
