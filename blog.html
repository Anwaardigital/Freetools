How to Compress Images Without Losing Quality

Uploading a photo and hitting a file-size limit is frustrating. Here's how to shrink images while keeping them sharp:

Step 1: Choose the right format

Photos compress best as JPG or WebP; graphics with transparency need PNG. Our Image Converter switches formats in seconds.

Step 2: Compress smartly

Open the Image Compressor, upload your image, and lower the quality slider gradually. For web use, 70–80% quality is usually indistinguishable from the original while cutting file size by more than half.

Step 3: Resize if needed

A 4000-pixel photo displayed at 800 pixels wastes bandwidth. Use the Image Resizer to match the display size first, then compress.

All processing happens in your browser — your photos are never uploaded anywhere.

Why Strong Passwords Matter (And How to Make Them)

"password123" is still one of the most common passwords in the world — and it can be cracked in under a second. Here's what actually keeps accounts safe:

Length beats complexity

A 16-character random password is vastly stronger than an 8-character "clever" one. Aim for at least 16 characters.

Never reuse passwords

When one site leaks (and sites leak constantly), reused passwords let attackers into your other accounts. Every account needs a unique password.

Let a generator do it

Our Password Generator creates cryptographically-random passwords instantly — no patterns for attackers to guess. Pair it with a password manager and you'll never memorize a password again.

Want to verify data integrity too? The Hash Generator creates SHA-256 hashes locally in your browser.
